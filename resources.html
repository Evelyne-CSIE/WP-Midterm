BEFORE YOUR NEXT STUDY SESSION

A simple study checklist

These steps do not need to take long. Preparing a little can make it easier to begin.

  1. Choose one topic or task.
  2. Prepare your notes and materials.
  3. Decide how many minutes to focus.
  4. Keep distractions away while you work.
  5. Review what you learned at the end.
